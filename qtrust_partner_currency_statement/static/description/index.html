Q-Trust Partner Currency Statement

A clearer view of customer and vendor balances in your selected currency

Review partner account movements for a selected company, partner, currency, and date range. See the opening balance, period debit and credit totals, and closing balance in one report.

How it works

1. Open the report

In Odoo, go to Accounting → Reporting → Foreign Currency Statement.

2. Choose your statement filters

Select the company, customer or vendor, currency, and reporting date range. Use the filters to focus on the account movements you need to review.

3. Review the balance and transactions

Review the opening balance, debit and credit totals for the selected period, closing balance, and the transaction rows available in the report. Use the row search to find a specific movement.

4. Export or print

Export statement lines to XLSX or print a PDF report for sharing and review.

Key features

  • Customer and vendor statement from the Accounting reporting menu.
  • Filter by partner, company, currency, and date period.
  • Opening balance, period debit and credit totals, and closing balance.
  • Search within the displayed transaction rows.
  • Export statement lines to XLSX and print a PDF report.
  • An additional reporting tool; it does not replace or modify standard accounting entries.
